🌿 Ayur Sutra – Panchkarma Patient Management & Therapy Scheduling App

A healthcare management web app built for **Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2023)** problem statement **SIH-25023**, published by the Ministry of Ayush.  
The app helps patients, doctors, and hospitals manage Panchkarma therapies, schedules, and health tips in a *modern, user-friendly* way.

---

## 🚀 Features
- 👨‍⚕️ Patient Management – Register, update, and track patient details.
- 📅 Therapy Scheduling – Book, update, and view Panchkarma therapy slots.
- 🔔 Reminder System – Notifications for hydration, exercise, or medicine intake.
- 🚑 SOS Button – Double press volume button → sends SMS to doctor & ambulance.
- 💊 Medication Tracker – Ongoing prescriptions displayed with reminders.
- 🧘 Health Tips & Warnings – Ayurvedic lifestyle guidance + food combination alerts.
- 🔍 Doctor Finder – Search specialists nearby.
- 🖥️ Admin Panel – Manage doctors, therapies, and hospital data.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js / HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
Backend: Node.js + Express.js  
Database: MySQL  
Other Tools: 
- Twilio / Fast2SMS (for SMS alerts)  
- Figma (UI/UX Design)  
- Postman (API testing)
